## How will you handle backdues?

I would make backdues one of my top priorities. My general approach is: prevent them when we can, make the process as easy and transparent as possible, follow up consistently, and treat termination as the absolute last resort after we’ve exhausted other options.

First, we should get people signed onto membership cards as early as possible so backdues don’t accumulate in the first place. Second, I’d work closely with the future CCS (Katherine!) to make sure DCSs and local stewards understand the dues situation in their areas/programs and can help with outreach. People are often much more receptive when they’re approached by someone they already know than by the VP suddenly appearing in their inbox.

I also want the process to be clearer and less confusing. We should be patient about explaining why dues matter, where the money goes, and how paying dues supports other members when they need the union. I’ve talked with both organizers and rank-and-file members about backdues, and my impression is that most people do understand the principle and are willing to pay when the situation is explained clearly. We should also be honest that our own systems used to be less organized, and recognize that admin-created problems have made some situations unnecessarily complicated.

At the same time, I do believe paying dues is part of our responsibility to one another as members. There’s a balance between making things accessible and still treating people like responsible adults. I won’t avoid difficult follow-ups or hard conversations when they’re necessary. As a boxer, I’m not afraid of confrontation, but martial arts also taught me that confrontation usually works best as a last resort, not a first instinct. It might make things even more difficult when people get defensive.

So my approach is basically: collaborate, be creative, be persistent, keep following up, and use every reasonable avenue before escalating. But when escalation really is necessary, don’t hesitate to do it.

And I also want to give credit to Yara, Maanasa, Katherine, and others who have already been putting a lot of work into improving the backdues system. I’m not assuming this will suddenly become an easy job. I just think the work they’ve already done gives us a stronger foundation, and my job would be to carry that forward and keep making the system better.
