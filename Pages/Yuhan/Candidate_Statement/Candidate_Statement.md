# Candidate Statement

Hi, my name is Yuhan Zhang, which means “lotus in the rain,” and stands for integrity in our culture. I am running for Vice President for Membership because I hope to help build a union that is organized, welcoming, and truly shaped by its members. What matters most to me is listening to people with different experiences and understanding what support actually looks like for them. Members should be able to find the information they need, bring forward their own ideas, and have a real say!

If elected, I will work with other graduate workers to make our union more connected and supportive. That means taking seriously the different challenges people face, whether that is funding insecurity, access to healthcare, or protections for international workers. As we bargain for our new contract, members should not just hear about what is happening after decisions are made. They should have meaningful ways to shape our priorities and take part in the process.

I will bring compassion, persistence, organization, and a willingness to listen and learn. Most of all, I hope we can build a union where people support one another and feel that this is truly something we create together.

For more information about me and my campaign please visit my campaign website [https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/](https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/) and feel free to email me at [YuhanZhang2029@u.northwestern.edu](YuhanZhang2029@u.northwestern.edu). Let’s grab a coffee and chat!
