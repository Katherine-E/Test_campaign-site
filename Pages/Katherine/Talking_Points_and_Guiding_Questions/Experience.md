## Do you have any experience that would help you do well in this role?

**I have been speaking with many past and present stewards and DCSs about what they want to see in the future of their union and in our organizing.** These connections and conversations give me incredibly valuable insight to what our elected officers and members want to see and how we as leaders on the executive board can better serve them. **I have also spoken to two past campus chief stewards, Peter and Maanasa to learn from their experiences as organizers and CCSs. Their insights on continuing to organize for our underrepresented divisions will set me up to hit the ground running as CCS and make sure that our McCormick and Weinberg divisions are NOT the only ones represented in our contract fight.**

As I spoke about above I have helped organize what will become the discipline and discharge working group after seeing how prevalent cases have become where administration utilizes loop holes to exclude workers from the bargaining unit and ultimately from their programs. **This organizing, its related petitions and grievances that I have been involved with in McCormick protect all workers, especially international students, activists for Palestinian liberation, those who refuse the biased “anti-bias training”, first year student struggling to get placed in labs, workers on leave who need to retain their healthcare, workers facing unfair practices surrounding qualifying exams or prospectus, the list goes on and on.** In addition to this organizing I have been part of a push alongside my current co-DCS Pallavi to file list exclusion grievances every quarter and have won several grievances to get Presidential and terminal year fellows in-unit. Pallavi and I have also successfully gotten **100% of in-unit work to sign a member or agency card in winter, spring and fall 2026, and I single handedly made connections to underrepresented departments to achieve this effort.**

Beyond my experience as a DCS and local steward and the many successful organizing efforts that I helped lead, **I also have years of experience running and organizing large organizations.** As an undergraduate I was vice president of my professional chemistry fraternity chapter which had almost a hundred active members. Through this leadership experience I became skilled at planning events, member outreach, recruitment, collaboration, communication, and conflict resolution. Additionally in 2022-2023 I was the president of a Michigan state wide youth service organization and was constantly in communication with members to organize fundraising, service events, recruitment, and facilitate connections to our organization in other states.
