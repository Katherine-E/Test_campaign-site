## Experienced UE negotiators like Kim Lawson provided instrumental guidance to the first BC, helping us win an exceptionally strong first contract (especially compared to the first contracts of peer unions like UIC). What’s so wrong about voting for other candidates who have a strong track record of heeding advice from UE organizers and/or GEB?

UEs advice and aid was vital in our first contract fight, and will prove vital again. **However since that first contract fight we have gained an immense amount of organizing experience, bargaining experience, and an intimate knowledge of what our members need. UE negotiators are not grad students living the day to day of our Northwestern workers, and while their experience is valuable we as an executive board must be willing to stand up for our workers** when UEs advice does not reflect what our workers need or our experience as a local. So yes while we will need the strength and support of UE **we have to be able to always put our workers first.**
