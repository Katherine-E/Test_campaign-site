## I have concerns about whether Katherine as a white woman would look out for my needs as a marginalized worker.

I hear you and acknowledge that I can never fully understand anyone else's perspective in life whether they look like me or not, every single one of us has a unique experience in this life and I will never assume to understand what someone else has experienced. **This is why I believe a large part of my job as CCS is listening to others, listening to our workers, listening to our stewards, listening to our DCSs, and even more importantly creating an environment of trust where our workers feel empowered to share their voice and their perspective. In order to look out for the needs of all of our workers, especially those from marginalized communities and identities we must make sure we ask them what those needs are, without assuming we have an understanding of them.** As elected leaders we cannot, and I will not, claim to be effective in my office if any worker feels silenced or that we will stop them from speaking up or organizing about what they care about.

## In the eyes of the Boss and the government, humanities & social science research has been far more undervalued than STEM research. Why should I vote for a STEM Campus Chief Steward instead of a H/SS Campus Chief Steward who would better represent this population of more vulnerable graduate workers?

As I said above **I believe a large part of my job as CCS is listening to others, listening to our workers, listening to our stewards, listening to our DCSs, and even more importantly creating an environment of trust where our workers feel empowered to share their voice and their perspective.** This also means making sure that we don't allow our union to reflect the attitude of the Boss and the government and undervalue our non-STEM workers. As a woman in a career built for men I understand how it feels to be underestimated and undervalued and I never want to make any of my fellow workers at Northwestern feel that way. **I refuse to be a CCS who lets the Boss and government dictate which of our workers matter, because they ALL matter and we as a union cannot use our collective power without ALL workers.**
