## Other candidates have much more experience as DCS compared to Katherine who's only been DCS for a couple of months. Why shouldn't I vote for the more experienced candidate here?

Time as DCS is not necessarily the best indicator for experience. **In just six months as McCormick DCS I have helped organize what will become the discipline and discharge working group after seeing how prevalent cases have become where administration utilizes loop holes to exclude workers from the bargaining unit and ultimately from their programs.** This organizing, its related petitions and grievances that I have been involved with in McCormick **protect all workers, especially international students, pro-palestinian activists, those who refuse anti-bias training, first year student struggling to get placed in labs, workers on leave who need to retain their healthcare, workers facing unfair practices surrounding qualifying exams or prospectus,** the list goes on and on. In addition to this organizing I have been part of a push alongside my current co-DCS Pallavi to **file list exclusion grievances every quarter and have won several grievances to get Presidential and terminal year fellows in-unit.** Pallavi and I have also **successfully gotten 100% of in-unit work to sign a member or agency card in winter, spring and fall 2026**, and I **single handedly made connections to underrepresented departments to achieve this effort.** It is all of these successful and sustained efforts of organizing that I have done as a DCS that make me the best candidate to be CCS. **Our workers who deserve a CCS will continue to fight for what matters to them and who will not rest until every single worker is protected, and who will work with every DCS and steward to make sure our organizing is successful, and I believe I have proven I can be that CCS.**
