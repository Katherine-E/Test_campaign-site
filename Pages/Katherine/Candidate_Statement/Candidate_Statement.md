# Candidate Statement

Hi! I’m Katherine (you’re welcome to call me Kate), and I’m running for Campus Chief Steward (CCS) because I am passionate about creating a better and safer work environment for all of us! I believe we are a stronger union when every worker is represented, has a voice, and is empowered to organize around issues they care about most. As CCS, my priorities will be to recruit stewards especially to our underserved divisions; to connect with and energize as many workers as possible to enable transparent, effective, and accessible bargaining; and to educate workers on their rights under our new contract so we can continue our ongoing campaign of grievances. As McCormick Division Chief Steward, I have learned and used the skills I need to make these priorities a reality. I organized around academic discipline and discharge to prevent NU from forcing workers to leave their programs, pursued bargaining-unit exclusion grievances, connected with under organized departments, and built membership participation across my division with 100% of in-unit cards signed each quarter of my term. As CCS, I will fight for transparent and member-driven bargaining. I will listen to the diverse needs of all workers, build a culture of open communication, and support member-led organizing efforts. I will fight to make sure no member is left behind. For more information about me and my campaign please visit my campaign website [https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/](https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/) and feel free to contact me via email at [katell@u.northwestern.edu](katell@u.northwestern.edu)
