## In the eyes of the Boss and the government, humanities & social science research has been far more undervalued than STEM research. Why should I vote for a STEM Campus Chief Steward instead of a H/SS Campus Chief Steward who would better represent this population of more vulnerable graduate workers?

The undervaluing of the humanities and social sciences, especially in recent years, has been absurdly frustrating to see. As a STEM researcher, I cannot say that I have experienced the same level of attacks that people in the humanities and social sciences have. But, I have and will continue to learn about how these attacks disproportionately affect studies besides my own. I have talked with many in the humanities and social sciences to learn about how the University weaponizes contract protections like bargaining unit status to deny humanities and social science researchers access to contract protections, 6th/7th year funding, and the international worker support fund. These denials are completely unjust, and I hope to continue fighting for and learning from humanities and social science researchers to give everyone the protections that they rightfully deserve as employees of Northwestern. 

I believe that all people should have a balance between science and the humanities. This divide between STEM and the humanities has been intentionally placed to cause each group to discredit the other, but it is a distraction. This divide is a distraction that is meant to cause us to lose faith in the other instead of fighting for what is best for the other. As president, I hope to end this divide and help all grad workers recognize that when the university attacks one area of research, they attack all areas of research.
