## Experienced UE negotiators like Kim Lawson provided instrumental guidance to the first BC, helping us win an exceptionally strong first contract (especially compared to the first contracts of peer unions like UIC). What’s so wrong about voting for other candidates who have a strong track record of heeding advice from UE organizers and/or GEB?

As our parent union, UE provides all of us with said instrumental guidance, not just Theo or Amanda. I believe that we should continue to have conversations with UE negotiators due to their many years of experience as well. However, it is important to recognize that the first contract was not won just because of the UE negotiators. Our strong first contract was won due to members like you participating and organizing alongside your peers. Our first contract was won because of a substantial effort done by our members. While UE’s guidance has greatly assisted our organizing capacity, we must also ensure that we are heeding the wishes of our membership first and foremost. As president, it will be my duty to understand UE’s suggestions as well as membership’s and make the choice that works best for our local, while maintaining the members’ wishes come first. Aside from that, I am very excited to work, to learn, and to organize with Alex and Sofia, who have both already shown the same passion and core values that we all have as well!
