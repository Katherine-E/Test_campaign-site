## Why are you choosing to not run with a slate? Don’t you think that going into the election with a full slate shows a level of commitment and shared values and ideals, and shows that the EB will function as a cohesive unit?

I believe that in NUGW, we all want what is best for our local. The differences we have are mainly in how we achieve those.  While I put my trust in the others I am running with, I still believe that slates inherently cause one question that bothers me. What happens if all elections are unopposed except for one? There becomes an inherent advantage in being a part of the slate, and the opposing candidate inherently becomes an “outsider.” But what if that “outsider” would bring in some fantastic ideas that no one on the slate had thought of before? Does this discredit the slate? In my opinion, it shouldn’t discredit the slate, but on a glance it could. I am opposed to the notion that this “outsider” is even an outsider at all. We are all just people trying to fight for what we believe to be good for our union. Call me optimistic (which I still fail to see how this is a bad thing), but I also do believe that our organizers and our members do have a similar set of shared values and ideals, and I remain unwilling to treat others in a way that shows otherwise. The commitment to a slate, at least to me, removes the idea that elections should remain on a personal basis. I will, of course, back those who I believe to be best for this union and campaign with them, but I also trust that membership has the final say in any decision that is made for our union. So, I am running without a slate to allow membership to make well-informed decisions with no prior bias that could be established through a slate’s implementation.
