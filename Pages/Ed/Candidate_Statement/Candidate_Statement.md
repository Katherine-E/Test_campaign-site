# Candidate Statement

Hello! My name is Edward Skrabacz, but you can call me Ed. I am running for President because all workers deserve a fair contract with strong protections, which can only be achieved through our collective power! As President, I want to achieve this by listening to our coworkers and inspiring them to fight for their rights.

Throughout my past two years, I have fought alongside my fellow workers to force the university to honor our contract, as well as inspiring other members to speak up along the way. As a Local Steward in Physics and Astronomy and a Divisional Chief Steward for Weinberg STEM, I have advocated for Northwestern to recognize internal fellows and expand our International Employee Support Fund to include passport renewal. Guiding stewards in my own division and beyond has enlightened me to the pressing issues facing workers across Northwestern. As President, I will use these valuable conversations to steer bargaining proposals that reflect the wishes of our membership. In these proposals, I will work to offer greater protections for international workers, to enshrine dependent healthcare for our parents and caregivers, and to force the university to guarantee funding across divisions for the entirety of a worker’s PhD.

When I began my PhD here at Northwestern, the striking passion from the workers in NUGW immediately drew me in. At my first meeting, I immediately resonated with the notion that “the members run this union.” As President, this will be my guiding light as I foster a community of open and honest communication where members are unafraid to voice their concerns. It is vital that *all* members have a say in their next contract, which can be achieved through regularly occurring bargaining updates, steward outreach, and transparency in the bargaining process. By properly fostering connection between all members, we will create a collective force to be reckoned with!

For more information about me and my campaign please visit my campaign website [https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/](https://katherine-e.github.io/Ed-Yuhan-Katherine-NUGW-Ticket/), or feel free to email me at [edward.skrabacz@u.northwestern.edu](edward.skrabacz@u.northwestern.edu), I’m always happy to chat :).
